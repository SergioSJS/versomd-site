Several notes open at the same time: in tabs, side by side, in separate workspaces or in windows of their own.

---

## Tabs

- **Preview tab**: a click on a note in the tree, in the search or in **Open…** opens a temporary tab, with the name in italics; the next click replaces it. A double click, an edit or pinning the tab keeps it. Turn it off under **Settings › Editor › Preview tabs**.
- **Pin tab** (the tab's menu): the tab shrinks to its icon, as in the browser, and does not close with **Close others**.
- A tab's menu (right click) also has **Open in new tab**, **Duplicate tab**, **Split right**, **Split down**, **Move tab to the next pane**, **Reveal in file tree**, **Rename**, **Move to…**, **Copy relative path** and the close options.

| Shortcut | Does |
| :--- | :--- |
| `⌘ W` | Close tab |
| `⌘ ⇧ T` | Reopen closed tab |
| `⌘ ⇧ ]` / `⌘ ⇧ [` | Next tab / Previous tab |
| `⌘ [` / `⌘ ]` | Back / Forward |

---

## Panes Side by Side

- `⌘\` splits right and `⌘⇧\` splits down.
- Or drag a tab to a pane's edge: the edge chooses the side; the middle puts the tab there.
- The **right panel** (outline, backlinks, tags, bookmarks, focuses and calendar) opens with the **Show right panel** button in the status bar, the button of the same name on the left ribbon, or with `⌘⌥→`. On a phone it is the **Panel** button of the bottom bar. The panel's tabs change place: drag a tab, or press `Alt+←` and `Alt+→` on a focused tab, or right-click › **Move left / Move right**; the order is kept. When there is no room (and, on Windows and Linux, the app draws its own window buttons in the corner), the tabs that do not fit go into the **⋯** button (**More panel tabs**).
- `⌘⇧M` maximizes the pane and `⌘⇧W` closes it.
- The **All tabs** button, in the corner of the tab bar, lists the pane's tabs and has **Vertical tabs** and **Hide tab bar** — a double click on the empty pane brings the bar back.

---

## Workspaces

A workspace is a set of panes and tabs, so you can switch subjects without closing anything.

- `⌘T` creates a new workspace; with two or more, the workspace bar appears and `⌘1` to `⌘9` switch between them.
- A double click on a workspace's name (or `F2`, or **Rename** on the right click) renames it.
- Drag a tab onto another workspace's name to take it there.

---

## Windows

- **No title bar**, on macOS, Windows and Linux: the window uses the whole height. On the Mac, the three system buttons sit in the top left corner; on Windows and Linux, minimize, maximize and close sit in the top right corner. Drag the window by the empty part of the tab bar, the top of the side bar or the ribbon; a double click there maximizes, as on a title bar. Nothing sits under the buttons, with the side bars open or closed.
- **New window** (the **File** menu, the palette or the ribbon) opens another window, on the home page, to open **another folder**: each window shows its own folder, with its name at the top of the side bar, in the Dock and in the taskbar. **Open folder…** in a window changes only that window's folder.
- **New window on this folder** (the palette, a tab's menu) opens another window on the same folder, with tabs of its own.
- **Open in a new window** (a tab's menu or a note's menu in the tree) takes the note to a window of its own; **Return to the main window** brings it back.
- `⌘R` reloads the window, saving the open notes first. If one cannot be saved (a conflict waiting for your choice), the window stays as it is and says why.
