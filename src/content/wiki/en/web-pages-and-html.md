A web page can be a note of yours, with annotations; an `.html` file in the folder opens as a page; and a page can appear in the middle of a note.

---

## A Page Saved as a Note

**New web page…** (in the menu of the `+` button on the left ribbon, or in a folder's menu) asks for the address and creates a note with it. Every note with the `url` property at the top is a page: the tab gets the **Back**, **Forward** and **Reload** bar.

- **Show page** and **Show note** switch between the site and the note's text — your annotations about it.
- The address, in the bar, opens the page in a window of its own.
- The star beside the title adds the page to your bookmarks, like a bookmark.

Open jsoncanvas.org to see one.

---

## HTML Files

An `.html` file in the folder opens as a page — without running scripts. In the corner of the tab, **Page** and **Source** switch between the page and the code. Open Business Card.html.

---

## A Page Inside a Note

An `<iframe>` embeds a page in the middle of a note; the first time a site appears, Verso asks before loading it. See how in [Documents and Media](/en/wiki/documents-and-media/#-web-pages-inside-the-note).

> **Note: Remote content**
>
> **Settings › General › Load remote content** decides whether web images and site icons load on their own. When it is off, nothing goes out to the network without a click from you.
