**Verso** is a note-taking and knowledge-management environment built around simplicity, speed, privacy and plain text. It uses local Markdown files compatible with the Obsidian ecosystem, with no proprietary databases and no hidden formats.

> **Note**
>
> This is a **Test and Demo Vault**. Feel free to explore the notes, change their contents, try out features and use this structure as a template for your own projects.

> **Tip: Start with the tour**
>
> Open the Verso Tour: a board with Verso's features, stop by stop, with this vault's notes inside the cards. To learn how to draw on boards, follow the Board Training.
>
> To follow a link: one click in **Reading** mode, or `⌘` + click in **Edit** mode — on the phone, tap the link and then **Open** ([Verso on Phone and Tablet](/en/wiki/verso-on-phone-and-tablet/)).

## 🚀 Featured Capabilities

In this vault you will find hands-on demos of all of Verso's main capabilities:

- **Interactive Checkboxes in Edit Mode**: Toggle tasks right in the text.
  - [x] Get to know Verso
  - [ ] Explore the demo notes
  - [ ] Try the Bases and Graph views
  - [ ] Try the two-page PDF reader
- **Quick Navigation with Wikilinks**: Internal links, links to specific sections and note transclusion.
- **Collapsible Properties Panel**: YAML metadata, organized and collapsible, at the top of the note.
- **Databases (`.base`)**: View and manage data in tables, Kanban boards and lists.
- **Interactive Graph Viewer**: Smooth zoom with the mouse wheel, trackpad pinch and fluid navigation.
- **Media and Document Support**: PDFs with side-by-side viewing (Spread Mode), images with scale control and safe iframes.

> **Tip**
>
> Switch between **Edit** mode and **Reading** mode with the shortcut `Cmd+E` (on macOS) or `Ctrl+E` (on Linux/Windows). You can also switch using the buttons in the top bar of each note.
